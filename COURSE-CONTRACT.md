# Year 9 Metalwork course contract

## Course and authority

- Course: Year 9 Metalwork — teacher-issued toolbox and fishing rod holder contexts.
- Year/stage: Year 9, Stage 5.
- Current authority: NSW Industrial Technology Years 7–10 Syllabus (2019), used for this existing course while schools plan for the new syllabus.
- Future reference: NSW Industrial Technology 7–10 Syllabus (2025), implemented from 2028, clearly separated from the current course.
- Source evidence: the repository’s existing core units 1–10, landing-page project contexts, teacher-directed exam, and the verified teacher-issued Tool Carryall and Fishing Rod Holder component drawings.

## Learning and evidence contract

- The student pathway is five two-week modules covering workshop systems and risk management; project briefs and constraints; materials, research and ideas; production planning and control; and testing, evaluation and folio completion.
- Every module includes concise theory, checked responses with feedback, a written evidence response, model feedback, identity fields, independent browser autosave, completion progress and Print / Save PDF. Module 2 also includes two plan-reading sections with ten checks each.
- The project folio contains exactly twelve editable evidence cards, each with a matching course-specific visual prompt, autosave and Print / Save PDF.
- Current and future syllabus references stay visibly distinct. Project-specific dimensions, assessment conditions and submission instructions come only from teacher-issued material.

## Project and safety boundaries

- The new hero and folio visuals are process-led illustrations, not authentic plans or claims about final geometry.
- Module 2 and the Resources section publish unchanged copies of two verified teacher-issued drawings with accessible full-page previews and larger PDF links.
- No dimensions, due dates, weightings, classroom codes or submission destinations are inferred.
- Machine processes, heat processes, joining and testing remain teacher-demonstrated and teacher-approved.

## Sister-site and release standard

- Direct, student-friendly landing page with a clear module pathway, evidence-ready interactions, purposeful workshop imagery, responsive layout and printable student output.
- Legacy Metalwork units remain available as source/reference material; the new pathway does not silently rewrite them.
- Release requires link and asset integrity, JavaScript checks, desktop and narrow-screen rendered QA, live GitHub Pages verification, and exact downstream link updates only after the canonical site is live.

## Exclusions

- The Timber barbecue carry-all drawing, imagery and week pages are not evidence for this Metalwork pathway and are deliberately excluded.
- The two verified Metalwork working drawings are published. Detailed assessment conditions, dates, weightings and submission destinations are not inferred from them.
- The site does not replace teacher demonstrations, risk assessment, supervision or issued task requirements.
