## Context verification

- The teacher `Tool Carryall Program` identifies the General Metal 1 introductory sheet-metal project and the body, ends and handle sequence. Its matching drawing names HANDLE, BODY and SIDE x 2. The site keeps its established `Sheet-metal Toolbox` label and states the different source title.
- The teacher `Rod Holder Program` describes the Fishing Rod Holder as a stake, rod-holder tube and rotation tab. The included components drawing names those same three parts and is marked `NOT TO SCALE`.

## Deliberately excluded candidates

- `Fishing rod stand v1.pdf` and `Fishing rod stand v1a.pdf` depict a different multi-rod stand/rack rather than the program's stake, tube and rotation-tab holder.
- `sheet metal order Body, Handle and Ends` files are workshop production stock layouts, not the student project drawing, and are not presented as the project plan.
- Timber carry-all drawings and imagery remain excluded from this Metal course.
